A clean, mathematically consistent rebalance of all vanilla and DLC ship weapons. No more assault cannons out-damaged by gatlings, or backwards accuracy—every weapon class follows clear rules for range, velocity, accuracy, and DPS with a well-defined role in combat.

[b]Want the exact numbers and vanilla comparisons?[/b] Check out the [url=https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/18wNjONzFTwHJaMPuOOFeCjLJrgL2vTV-/edit?usp=sharing]Full Weapon Rebalance Spreadsheet[/url].

[h2]Why This Mod Exists[/h2]
Vanilla Space Engineers weapon stats were tuned one block at a time over the years, leaving several awkward quirks in ship combat:
[list]
[*][b]Artificially Short Turret Ranges:[/b] Vanilla turret AI capped engagement at [b]800 m[/b] (Large Grid) and [b]600 m[/b] (Small Grid), even when Assault Cannon shells flew [b]1,400 m[/b] and Artillery shells flew [b]2,000 m[/b].
[*][b]Broken DPS Ladder & Fixed/Turret Gap:[/b] Fixed gatlings and autocannons never pause to reload—out-damaging their turrets by [b]30–56%[/b]—while the single-barrel Assault Cannon's 6 s reload left it dealing less sustained DPS ([b]700[/b]) than a Gatling ([b]792–1,050[/b]).
[*][b]Backwards Accuracy:[/b] Artillery turrets ([b]0.30°[/b]) and Assault Cannon turrets ([b]0.35°–0.50°[/b]) had looser spread than Autocannons ([b]0.25°[/b]) and Gatlings ([b]0.225°[/b]).
[*][b]Sluggish Point Defense:[/b] Vanilla Gatling turrets fired at [b]700 RPM[/b] with slow tracking and 10-tick update intervals, making missile and torpedo interception unreliable.
[*][b]Wide Launch Arcs:[/b] Homing missiles and torpedoes turned at a fixed [b]6–25°/s[/b] while accelerating, so anything fired off-axis (like a vertical torpedo tube) swung out in a [b]500–1,150 m[/b] arc before tracking its target, and homing missiles could barely hit anything up close.
[/list]

[h2]Core Design Rules[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Extended Ranges & Realistic Velocities (3 km Cap):[/b]
  [list]
  [*]Every weapon reaches further up to the [b]3,000 m[/b] multiplayer sync and turret-update ceiling while keeping vanilla's short-to-long engagement order.
  [*]Turret targeting ranges match projectile ranges, with Small Grid turrets keeping vanilla's [b]75%[/b] ratio. Custom Turret Controllers are raised to [b]3,000 m[/b] (LG) / [b]2,000 m[/b] (SG).
  [*]Gun and shell velocities are raised toward real muzzle speeds so time-to-target stays close to vanilla ([b]~1 s[/b] for guns, [b]~3.8 s[/b] for artillery).
  [*]Missile and torpedo top speeds and acceleration are [b]unchanged[/b] so point defense stays effective—extra range comes from extended flight time and seeker guidance range.
  [/list]
[*][b]Tight Missile & Torpedo Launch Turns:[/b] Homing missiles and torpedoes leave the launcher slowly and steer much harder, so they swing onto their target right after launch—even from vertical tubes or at targets 90° off the launcher—then build up to their normal top speed.
  [list]
  [*][b]Launch Speed:[/b] Homing Missile 100 → 30 m/s; all torpedoes → 10 m/s (they still inherit the launching ship's velocity).
  [*][b]Turn Rate:[/b] Homing Missile 16 → 100°/s; Torpedo 14 → 45°/s; Heavy 6 → 25°/s; Light 25 → 70°/s; Armor-Piercing 12 → 40°/s.
  [/list]
[*][b]Consistent DPS Ladder & Fixed/Turret Parity:[/b] Fixed guns and single-barrel turrets of the same caliber deal equal sustained DPS, while double-barrel Large Grid turrets keep ~2x single-barrel output. Ammo cost per damage stays vanilla across all weapons.
  [list]
  [*][b]Gatling:[/b] ~803–806 DPS (fixed 900 RPM; turrets 1,200 RPM w/ 4 s reload)
  [*][b]Autocannon:[/b] 1,000 DPS (fixed 120 RPM; turrets 150 RPM w/ 2 s reload)
  [*][b]Assault Cannon:[/b] 1,200 DPS single-barrel / 2,191 DPS LG turret (reload 6 s → 3.5 s)
  [*][b]Artillery:[/b] 1,458 DPS fixed / 2,745 DPS LG turret (unchanged)
  [/list]
[*][b]Caliber-Consistent Accuracy:[/b] One spread value per caliber across fixed mounts and turrets, ordered realistically:
  [b]Artillery (0.15°) > Assault Cannon & Autocannon (0.20°) > Gatling (0.25°)[/b]
  Tuned for a [b]5–8 m[/b] miss radius at max range so large ships are reliably hit while small fighters stay evasive. [i](Railguns 0°, Anti-Personnel 0.15°, and Rockets 0.50° are unchanged.)[/i]
[*][b]Dedicated Point-Defense Gatlings:[/b] Turrets fire at [b]1,200 RPM[/b] (was 700) for [b]53.55 dmg/round[/b] (destroying 160-integrity torpedoes in 3 hits) with [b]240 rounds/box[/b] (was 140), keeping vanilla DPS, burst duration (12 s), recoil, and ingot cost per damage—paired with [b]2x faster[/b] turret tracking and 3-tick targeting updates.
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility & Details[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]All DLC Variants & Tooltips Updated:[/b] Includes vanilla, Warfare 2, Warfare 3 (Type II), and Contact Pack reskins, and updates in-game block descriptions with the rebalanced range, rate of fire, and damage stats.
[*][b]Optional JS - Weapon Overhaul Support:[/b] Automatically applies matching range, accuracy, reload, and tooltip tuning to [b]JS - Weapon Overhaul[/b] weapons (Large Autocannon Turret, Small Artillery, Railgun Turret) if loaded, and runs cleanly standalone without it.
[*][b]Powered by ModAdjuster:[/b] Uses field-level [b]ModAdjusterV2[/b] patches (plus a lightweight world-load script for missile lifetime, launch speed, turn rate, seeker guidance range, and optional-mod detection) rather than overwriting full block definitions, keeping it compatible with mods that change weapon visuals, effects, or build components. [i](Requires ModAdjusterV2.)[/i]
[*][b]Safe to Add or Remove:[/b] Does not add or remove blocks and restores runtime-tuned fields on unload, so it can be added to or removed from existing saves safely.
[/list]
