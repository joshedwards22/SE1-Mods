A clean, lightweight economy tweak that removes Uranium and Platinum requirements from all heavy ammunition recipes—replacing them with Cobalt and Gold so late-game munitions stay craftable without Uranium or Platinum bottlenecks.

[hr][/hr]

[h2]Recipe Changes[/h2]
Kinetic/armor-piercing Uranium costs are replaced with [b]Cobalt[/b], while guidance and propulsion Uranium/Platinum costs are replaced with [b]Gold[/b]. All other ingredients (Iron, Nickel, Silicon, Magnesium) remain vanilla:
[list]
[*][b]Homing Missile:[/b] -0.1 Uranium, -0.04 Platinum → [b]+0.32 Gold[/b]
[*][b]Artillery Shell:[/b] -0.1 Uranium → [b]+3 Cobalt[/b]
[*][b]Small Railgun Sabot:[/b] -0.2 Uranium → [b]+6 Cobalt[/b]
[*][b]Large Railgun Sabot:[/b] -1 Uranium → [b]+30 Cobalt[/b]
[*][b]Standard Torpedo:[/b] -1 Uranium, -1 Platinum → [b]+5 Gold[/b]
[*][b]Light (Seeking) Torpedo:[/b] -1 Uranium, -2 Platinum → [b]+8 Gold[/b]
[*][b]Heavy Torpedo:[/b] -2 Uranium, -1 Platinum → [b]+7 Gold[/b]
[*][b]Armor-Piercing Torpedo:[/b] -3 Uranium, -1 Platinum → [b]+5 Gold, +60 Cobalt[/b]
[/list]
