A complete expansion and visual polish pass for Space Engineers conveyors. Fills in missing elbows, crosses, junctions, and airtight variants across every conveyor family while fixing vanilla port visuals and rotation quirks.

[hr][/hr]

[h2]Features & Fixes[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Complete Shape Coverage:[/b] Adds 19 missing blocks so every conveyor family has a full set of shapes—including elbows, crosses, and medium-tube junctions.
[*][b]Airtight Large Conveyors:[/b] Adds airtight Straight, Curved, T, Elbow, and Cross variants in the Conveyor Junction style so you can run airtight lines without capping unused ports.
[*][b]Polished Visuals:[/b] Fixes conveyor ends to look like proper conveyor ports rather than hollow holes, and updates Small and Medium Conveyor Tubes to match the Large Conveyor Tube art style.
[*][b]Free Rotation:[/b] Fixes vanilla rotation restrictions so conveyor blocks can be oriented freely in any direction.
[/list]
